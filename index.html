What I work on

Three connected strands of research

Applied microeconomics and causal inference, from randomised field experiments to large administrative and geospatial datasets — consistently aimed at questions policymakers are actually asking.

01

Environment & resource economics

Cumulative climate shocks and migration in Sub-Saharan Africa, adaptive capacity under repeated drought, and the local economics of mining and natural-resource extraction.

See publications
02

Supply chains & sustainable transformation

Digital traceability for deforestation-free cocoa in Ghana, tested through a pre-registered field experiment, and the policy leverage points that shift finance and trade.

See current projects
03

Institutions & development

Firm selection under weak institutions, development assistance and crime-accused politicians in Indian elections, and horizontal inequalities in the labour market.

See policy work
